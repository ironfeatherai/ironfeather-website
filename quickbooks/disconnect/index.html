QuickBooks Integration

Disconnect QuickBooks

You can remove IronFeather AI's access to your Intuit QuickBooks Online company at any time. No notice, no penalty, and no need to ask us first.

Your connection may already be disconnected. If you arrived here after disconnecting inside QuickBooks, the change takes effect immediately. Nothing further is required from you.

Option 1 — Disconnect inside QuickBooks

This revokes access immediately and is the fastest route.

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Open the Apps menu.
  3. Select My Apps.
  4. Find IronFeather AI in the list.
  5. Choose Disconnect and confirm.

Option 2 — Ask us to disconnect

Email hello@ironfeather.ai and we will revoke the connection and delete the stored token, normally within one business day. We will confirm in writing once it is done.

Request disconnection

What happens when you disconnect

  • Our access to your QuickBooks company stops immediately.
  • Stored access and refresh tokens are deleted.
  • No further invoices, customers, or records are read or written by us.
  • Records already created in your QuickBooks company remain yours and stay in place.
  • Any automated bookkeeping tasks we performed for you stop.

Your data after disconnection
