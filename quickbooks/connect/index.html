QuickBooks Integration

Connect your QuickBooks company

IronFeather AI can connect to your Intuit QuickBooks Online company to prepare and file invoices, keep customer records current, and reduce manual bookkeeping entry. The connection is authorized by you and can be removed by you at any time.

Connections are set up with you directly. IronFeather is an operator-led service, not self-serve software. Your account manager arranges the connection during onboarding so scope and permissions are agreed before anything is authorized.

How to connect or reconnect

  1. Contact your IronFeather account manager, or email hello@ironfeather.ai.
  2. We confirm which QuickBooks company to connect and what tasks you want handled.
  3. We send you an Intuit authorization link.
  4. You sign in to QuickBooks and approve the connection. Intuit — not IronFeather — handles your sign-in.
  5. We confirm the connection is active and outline what happens next.

Reconnecting follows the same steps. A reconnection is sometimes needed if authorization expires or if the company file changes.

What we access

We request accounting scope only, and we use the narrowest access needed:

  • Customers and contact records
  • Invoices, line items, and tax codes
  • Payment status on invoices we manage
  • Company information needed to apply the correct tax treatment

We do not access payroll, banking credentials, or unrelated financial records. We never use your accounting data for advertising, profiling, resale, or to train AI models.

Your credentials

IronFeather never sees or stores your QuickBooks username or password. Authorization happens on Intuit's own sign-in page. We hold only an access token, encrypted at rest, which you can revoke at any time.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect whenever you like — see how to disconnect.
